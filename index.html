Share tracker

Every transaction that changes share counts is shown here in order, so you can see exactly how outstanding shares are calculated at any point in time. Outstanding shares are what determine common dividends.

Dividend calculator

Shows every declared dividend fully worked out — preferred first, then common — with the exact share counts and formulas used. The most common exam mistake is using issued shares instead of outstanding shares for common dividends.

Journal entries

Every transaction generates a properly formatted journal entry with debits on the left, credits indented on the right, and the math shown for each line.

T-accounts

Each account shows its opening balance, every transaction posted to it, and the ending balance. New entries appear highlighted in blue.

Balance sheet — equity section

The final stockholders' equity section, formatted exactly as it would appear on a real balance sheet. Updates live as you add or change transactions.
